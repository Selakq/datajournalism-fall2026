For this assignment, I chose to critique the article Lawless: One in three Alaska villages have no local police by Kyle Hopkins. The hyperlink can be found below.

I would give this article a 5 in terms of having clear insight and clarity for the reader. The headline is clear, captivating and provides context for the reader. I also think the anecdotal lede style works really well for this article because it provides context while also introducing the larger topic. 

There is one example of data visualization in this article. This article features a photo of Alaska map style with red dots signifying the communities with no local law enforcement in 2019. I would give this chart a 4 rating. Visually, it is easy to see each individual red dot and the county they are referring to.However, similar to how voting maps are not completely accurate, this map does not show the size of each town or population. This map also does not compare the amount of towns with no enforcement to the towns with local law enforcement. I think the chart could be improved by providing the additional information I mentioned before and to have a key on the side comparing each towns population. I believe these two aspects are important because including dots signifying towns with local law enforcement can help readers visually see how nearby or far out law enforcement is from the towns mentioned in the article. 

The first source I would question would be the person the journalist interviewed who claimed she is the only local law enforcement within 100 sq miles of her town. I wonder how she knows that for certain. Unfortunately, the census links on the article that gave specific numerical data about each towns population and demographics are now unavailable. I wonder what data has changed since this article was written in 2019 about the size of towns and the number of official local law enforcement in the area. However, this article did have a large number of sources and data, including the no longer available census. The journalist cited various lawsuits, citizen experiences, the local law enforcement officer, government documents and more. In my opinion, I think the journalist did explain everything needed for this article. In fact, I believe one could even say they went above and beyond by exploring different towns and the different ways a lack of law enforcement can impact a town. 


I believe this article displays a high level of reporting and would give it a 5. The article was able to explain stories about how towns deal with lack of local law enforcement from both the citizen and law enforcement perspective, and also provided strong background information.
