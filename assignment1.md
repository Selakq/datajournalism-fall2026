For this assignment, I chose to critique the article Lawless: One in three Alaska villages have no local police by Kyle Hopkins. The hyperlink can be found below. 

https://github.com/Selakq/datajournalism-fall2026/new/main

I would give this article a 5 in terms of having clear insight and clarity for the reader. The headline is clear and captivating and provides context for the reader I also think the anecdotal lede style works really well for this story because it provides context but also tells a story about a specific person and town. This article also speaks to the broader state of Alaska's lack of police in certain towns. Overall, this article was both clear and interesting in the way it was written.

The data visualization in this article is a photo of Alaska's shape on a map with red dots signifying the communities with no local law enforcement in 2019. I would give this chart a 3.5 rating. Visually, it is easy to see each individual red dot and area and the text has a key on how to read the chart. However, similar to how voting maps are not completely accurate, this map does not show the size of each town and does not compare the amount of towns with no enforcement to the towns with local law enforcement. I think the chart could be improved by providing the additional information I mentioned before.

The first source I would question would be the person the journalist interviewed who claimed she is the only local law enforcement within 100 sq miles of her town. I wonder how she knows that. The census links on the article are now unavailable, so I wonder what data has changed since this article was written in 2019 about the size of towns and the number of official local law enforcement in the area. However, this article did have a large number of sources and data, including the no longer available census. The journalist cited various lawsuits, citizens, the local law enforcement officer, government documents and more. In my opinion, I think the journalist did explain everything that needed explaining and provided the proper sources and context for the reader to understand it.


I believe this article displays a high level of reporting and would give it a 5. Outside of the story being interesting, the article was able to explain not only a stories about how towns deal with lack of local law enforcement from both the citizen and law enforcement perspective, but also provided strong background information. The visuals made sense with the story, and the sources and citations in the article elevated the story. I read this as a more anecdotal piece, and believe the storytelling married very well with the reporting of the issue. It did not feel like there were any filler paragraphs or any information that would be difficult for a reader to understand. In terms of critiques, this article told many stories at once and felt like it could be many different articles at a point. Although each story revolved around the impacts of lack of law enforcement, it became a lot of information at once which could overwhelm certain readers. I noticed this when the article went a bit back and fourth between stories about the town to different political decisions for multiple different stories. 

The graphic included was a map of Alaska with red dots signifying each town without local law enforcement nearby. As I mentioned before, there is a key to understand the map and it was clear. If the map wanted to display more information, I would add blue dots to represent the towns with local law enforcement to the red dots in order to numerically and visually see the difference. It was a simple and effective map. I would rate it a 4 for type and a 5 for color. Easy to see/understand, but could have more information. 

I believe the overall piece was creative in story and way of reporting. Something that stood out to me was the article also had links to videos and photos of the people and places whose stories the journalist was telling. Through these videos and photos, the reader has a visual of each person's expierence. I believe these additions elevated the quotes, data and overall reporting the journalist included because the reader can see and hear those aspects as well. It also is not seen often since the videos cannot translate to print, but I think it is very effective. I would rate the creativity a 5 because of how it brings a new layer/side to the article.
