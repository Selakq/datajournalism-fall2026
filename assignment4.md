Instead of reading the data in table form, I chose to use a bar chart. I thought it would help visualize the caffeine difference per drink and per chain while also highlighting the difference in ml for the chains that offered the same drink. I also think it clearly distinguishes each separate product even if it is from the same cafe. Ideally, I would have wanted to do a stacked bar chart that would show just the drinks and then divide the bar based on product, but it looked messier than anticipated and I thought this was the next best option.

Although similar to the original data set, I think created two separate bars distinguishing for the men and women for each drink make the data easier to read and easier to compare. in the original, it is hard to see any real significant difference because the bars go out to the sides and only have a numerical label. In my version, you can read and see the difference in the percentage per drink and per gender side to side. I thought this was better because it seperates each catagory while still showing the comparisons between gender and drinks. I also used different colors to distinguish men and women for each catagory, unlike the previous data set. even though in the original men and women are separated left and right, I think the additional label could be helpful since each drink category used the same color for the bar graph. I chose to use just the drink type to label my graph, and different colors for men and women to keep consistent clarity for the whole chart.
