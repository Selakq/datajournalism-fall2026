This particular data set displays the attendance for each WNBA's average attendance. This includes audience members that are physically in the arena despite the team they are there to support. It does not include streaming. It does NOT include the average ticket price per game, which I sought out and added the data for. 

The database was created by researchers who created a fan-run database "Across the timeline." The website is a database for the WNBA from player statistics, coach's statistics, and game attendance. Across the timeline has data from the very first WNBA game to the most recent. Across the timeline gets their data by manually aggregating information from the box scores, player stats, game archives, press releases and other public WNBA game information. 

I specifically chose to focus on the average game attendance for each WNBA team in the 2026 season. This database covers every WNBA game for each team both home and away. The dataset includes the least attended game as well as the highest attended game. A current limitation of this data set is that the 2026 season is not over and this data will change overtime. Across the timeline collects their data off of ticket sales. This data does not include viewers from home.

The reporting question I want to answer is "what is the average ticket price per WNBA game and does it differ between teams?" The data I found for the ticket prices was found on a website called Bookies that created a price index based off of the 2026 data. In the grand total on their table, transport, food, and drink are also included. I will only be taking their data from the average "4x" ticket price before the additional purchases. "4x" is the total of the 4 cheapest tickets. This website did not have the data for those individual prices, and I could not find one that did. Bookie's website averaged the prices so seats that are more expensive such as courtside and lower level wont be highlighted. The same goes for the nosebleed seats, which are less expensive. This chart does not include the average scalper price.

This information is newsworthy because the WNBA is still building their league. Statistics like this track their successes and growth. Many players are making a name for themselves, and as those players become more known, ticket prices and attendance will hopefully show that success. It is important to note the women's team often plays in a slightly smaller arena than the men's team of the same state, which impacts initial prices and attendance. However, more popular games such as Fever v. Sky or Mytics v. Fever are often played in the mens arena. This data does not separate those instances. 

This data shows that teams with "franchise players" such as the Indiana Fever are more likely to have high attendance and high ticket prices. It can also be assumed that when other teams play the Fever, that their average attendance will also go up. I also believe that as the WNBA 2026 season continues and more people get into the WNBA, more and more people will go to the games with cheaper tickets and eventually, the WNBA will have more standard pricing and increased audiences every game.
